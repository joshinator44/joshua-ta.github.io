SYSTEMS · OPEN SOURCE

Kalshi Matching Engine

Rebuilt the matching core of a prediction-market exchange in C++17 to learn how trades actually execute: price-time priority order book with limit and market orders, O(1) cancellation, an MPSC queue feeding the matcher, and a TCP gateway. Moving to integer-tick pricing and removing allocations from the hot path got it to 2.87M orders/sec at ~348 ns median latency (my machine, so treat as relative).
